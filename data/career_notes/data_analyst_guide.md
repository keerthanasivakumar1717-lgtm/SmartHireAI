# Career Guide: Transitioning to Data Analyst

*Author / Source: SmartHire Career Knowledge Base (Curated User-Editable Guide)*

## Role Overview
A Data Analyst translates raw numbers into actionable business insights. Typical responsibilities include data extraction, data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), metric tracking, and building visual dashboards for non-technical stakeholders.

## Core Technical Skills Required
1. **SQL (Structured Query Language):**
   - SELECT queries, filtering with `WHERE`, aggregations (`GROUP BY`, `HAVING`).
   - Table joins (`INNER JOIN`, `LEFT JOIN`, `FULL OUTER JOIN`).
   - Window functions (`ROW_NUMBER()`, `RANK()`, `LEAD()`, `LAG()`).
   - Subqueries and Common Table Expressions (CTEs).

2. **Data Manipulation in Python or R:**
   - **Pandas:** DataFrame operations, filtering, grouping, merging, handling null values.
   - **NumPy:** Vectorized calculations and numerical summaries.
   - **Visualization:** Matplotlib and Seaborn for trend charts, distributions, and heatmaps.

3. **Business Intelligence (BI) & Dashboarding:**
   - **Tableau** or **Power BI**: Connecting to datasources, calculated fields, parameter controls, interactive filtering, and executive summaries.

4. **Spreadsheet Proficiency (Excel / Google Sheets):**
   - Pivot tables, VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP, INDEX-MATCH, conditional formatting, basic statistical functions.

## Transition Strategy for Beginners
- **Step 1: Master SQL First.** 70% of data analyst interview screening revolves around SQL query syntax and data extraction logic.
- **Step 2: Build 2-3 Public Portfolio Projects.** Rather than using standard Titanic or Iris datasets, find real-world business datasets on Kaggle or government open data portals (e.g., e-commerce churn, hospital admissions, or retail sales). Document your problem statement, data cleaning steps, business insights, and recommendations in a clear GitHub README.
- **Step 3: Develop Business Acumen.** Analysts do not just report numbers; they explain "why" a metric changed (e.g., why customer churn spiked by 12% last quarter).

## Interview Preparation Checklist
- Be prepared for a live SQL coding round (often involving joins, group-bys, and date filtering).
- Be ready to walk through a dashboard you built: Explain how you chose your charts and what business action was taken based on the insights.
- Know how to handle messy data: dealing with duplicates, inconsistent date formats, and missing values.
